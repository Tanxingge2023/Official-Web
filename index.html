About our team

TanXG was founded in Aug. 2023. The name TanXG is derived from the pinyin initials of its Chinese name "探星阁".

We have lots of columns in our website. Here you will find various lecture notes on physics, astronomy/astrophysics as well as mathematics written by TanXG fellows. What's more, we also provide articles about popular science, historical development of physics and astronomy, and some learning and growing experiences written by us too. In addation, some of our fellows are also ambassadors of IAAC (International Astronomy and Astrophysics Competition), therefore we will also provide you with some information about this excellent competiton! We believe that in the future, more and more columns and contents will be added.

However, it should be noted that TanXG is currently run by university students with limited experience. Therefore, all resources and materials on this site are for reference only. We welcome corrections and suggestions!

Besides, we sincerely welcome you to join TanXG team and community. Our contact email is: tanxg2023@163.com
