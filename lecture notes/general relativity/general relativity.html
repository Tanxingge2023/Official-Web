General Relativity Slides(CN)

Chinese lecture slides for a beginner-friendly general relativity seminar.

This is the course slides used by D. Liang for the General Relativity seminar organized on campus in the spring semester of 2026. Designed for beginners, this seminar builds a platform for collective learning and academic communication. It focuses on fundamental knowledge of general relativity through discussion and sharing, prioritizing physical concepts and intuitive physical pictures rather than rigorous mathematical formalism.

The slides contain detailed written content to facilitate independent study. The course outline is as follows:

Chapter 1: Special Relativity
Chapter 2: Fundamentals of Differential Geometry (Ⅰ)
Chapter 3: Fundamentals of Differential Geometry (Ⅱ)
Chapter 4: Einstein Field Equations
Chapter 5: Schwarzschild Spacetime
Chapter 6: Black Holes
Chapter 7: Gravitational Waves
Chapter 8: Cosmology
