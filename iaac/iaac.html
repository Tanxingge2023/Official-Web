IAAC

The International Astronomy and Astrophysics Competition (IAAC) is an online science competition for middle school/college students around the world who are interested in astronomy, astrophysics, and the universe. Some of TanXG fellows are IAAC ambassadors, and we are here to introduce you this excellent competition!

IAAC provides astronomy and astrophysics problems that are rather different from what you used to deal with in school: they combine basic knowledge/concepts, math calculation, research frontier, and curiosity-driven learning. And the competition is designed to be accessible internationally: students can participate online and work through challenging problems with only basic tools and an internet connection.

For students who enjoy astronomy, astrophysics, cosmology as well as scientific problem solving, IAAC can be a good starting point for building confidence and discovering deeper topics in these disciplines.

For more information about IAAC, please visit the official website. If you want some learning materials for preparation or reference, one good idea is to check out TanXG lecture notes on astronomy and astrophysics. You are also sincerely welcomed to contact the ambassadors (D.Liang) for academic discussion and learning together!
